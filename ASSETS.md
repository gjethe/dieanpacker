## Replacing stock photos with real photos

1. Put the new image in `public/images/` (JPG/WebP, at least 1600 px wide; `next/image` creates the smaller sizes).
2. Change the `src` and `alt` in `content/services.ts` (services), `content/posts-a.ts` / `posts-b.ts` (articles) or `app/page.tsx` / `app/ueber-uns/page.tsx`.
3. Remove the "Symbolbild" caption where the photo shows real Die Anpacker work, and update this file and the Impressum's Bildnachweis.
