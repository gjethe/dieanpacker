## What competitors do well

- **Exact-match titles** ("Umzug Graz", "Umzugsfirma Graz") and district lists. Die Anpacker already has strong titles; district names are used naturally on service pages and articles, not as lists.
- **Visible social proof:** testimonials, founder faces, counts. Die Anpacker has none it can publish yet (see below).
- **Booking a Besichtigung** as a named CTA (umzuggraz.at). Die Anpacker offers viewings "bei Bedarf" in the process copy.
- **WhatsApp + callback** (moebelpacker-graz.at). Die Anpacker already has phone, WhatsApp, e-mail, form with photos and a mobile contact bar.

## Gaps Die Anpacker now covers better

- **Depth of useful information.** None of the inspected sites has more than three articles; most have none. Die Anpacker now has 18 sourced articles that answer the pre-purchase questions (costs, comparing quotes, Kostenvoranschlag law, disposal, handover, business address change).
- **Direct answers + structured data.** Answer-first blocks on every service page and article, consistent entity data in JSON-LD and llms.txt. Competitors mostly rely on marketing claims.
- **Honesty as a differentiator.** Competitors advertise "Fixpreis", "keine versteckten Kosten", "7/24", struck-through prices and unsourced counts. Die Anpacker explains how prices arise and what a quote must contain, which fits searchers comparing offers (article 9).
- **Disposal clarity.** No competitor explains where cleared items go. Article 16 does, with official Graz sources.
- **Performance and privacy.** Static pages, no trackers, no third-party requests; several competitors run WordPress builders.

## Gaps that remain (client input needed)

| Gap | What is needed |
|---|---|
| No reviews/testimonials | Real Google reviews on the Business Profile; written permission from customers to quote them (name or initials, date, service). Do not import or paraphrase reviews from elsewhere. |
| No team/owner presence | A photo and short bio of the owner/team for Über uns (strong E-E-A-T signal, also for AI answers). |
| No real job photos | Own photos of vehicle, team at work (with consent). |
| No confirmed experience/insurance | If true and provable: founding year, insurance cover, Gewerbe. Currently omitted on purpose. |
| No price orientation | Competitors publish hourly rates. If the client wants to publish rates, they must be real and current; then a price section could be added to service pages. |

## Search intent observations (autocomplete, AT)

- "umzug graz …" mixes events (Faschingsumzug, Krampusumzug) with moving; "umzugsfirma/umzugsunternehmen graz" is clean. Homepage targets the latter (unchanged).
- "wohnung räumen …" is dominated by legal questions (Erbe ausschlagen, Todesfall, Vermieter). Article 11 and the existing Verlassenschaft article answer the practical side and point to the Gerichtskommissär/AK for legal ones.
- "keller entrümpeln …": "wo anfangen", "wohin mit dem müll", "kosten", "tipps" → article 12 headings follow these.
- "sperrmüll graz …": Öffnungszeiten, Abholung, Kosten, Graz-Umgebung → article 16 explains routes and links the official pages instead of copying fees that change.
- "umzugsangebot …": einholen, vergleichen, verbindlich, Muster → article 9.
- "kurzfristig umziehen", "umzug im alter", "umzug was alles ummelden österreich" → articles 13, 17, 18 + existing checklist.
