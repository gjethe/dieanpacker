## Deviations from the suggested briefs, and why

- **Article 14** was suggested as "Firmenübersiedlung planen: Eine Checkliste für Unternehmen". The existing article `/blog/firmenumzug-graz-planen` already is that checklist (timeline, responsibilities, IT, labelling, moving day). A second one would compete for the same query. The new article covers the part the existing one only mentions in two sentences: address change, Gewerbebehörde, Firmenbuch, where the address appears, customer communication. It links to the existing article for logistics, and vice versa.
- **Article 11** focuses on clearing a flat *before a handover* (Mietende, Verkauf, move to care), including the ABGB § 1109 return rule. "Wohnungsräumung Graz" as a transactional term stays with the Entrümpelung service page.
- **Article 16** focuses on *where things go* (waste categories, Ressourcenpark zones, Graz-Umgebung via Gemeinde/AWV, data on paper files), while the existing Entrümpelung article keeps the process.
- **Article 10** names real Graz-Umgebung municipalities only as orientation and states plainly that there is no office outside Graz.

## Structure: not one template

Each article has its own shape, matched to its job: a 4-step method plus checklist table (9); a "what's different in the Umland" comparison (10); three starting situations plus a numbered timeline (11); a sorting method and DIY-vs-hire decision list (12); a strict priority order 1–5 (13); an authority-by-authority section plus a "where your address appears" table (14); an object-by-object preparation guide (15); a waste-category table plus local disposal routes (16); a people-first planning guide (17); a day-by-day countdown (18).

Common elements (because they help readers and answer engines, not as a formula): an answer-first "Kurz gesagt" block, question-shaped H2s where natural, an FAQ of 3–4 real questions, sources where official rules are mentioned, a closing paragraph with links to the matching service(s) and the contact page.
