## Findings that shaped the strategy

1. **"Umzug Graz" is intent-mixed.** Autocomplete shows "Krampusumzug", "Faschingsumzug", "St. Martin Umzug" next to commercial terms. Therefore the homepage targets "Umzugsfirma Graz" / "Umzugsunternehmen Graz" (unambiguous) while still using "Umzug in Graz" naturally.
2. **Austrian vocabulary matters.** "Übersiedlung/übersiedeln", "Übersiedlungsfirma Graz", "Möbeltaxi Graz", "Stiege", "Kasten", "Lift", "Erdgeschoß" appear in autocomplete or are standard in AT. Used naturally in copy.
3. **Price intent is strong.** "umzug graz kosten", "umzugsfirma graz kosten/günstig/preis", "entrümpelung graz kosten/preise", "was kostet ein umzug", "wie viel kostet eine übersiedlung" → dedicated cost article without fake prices.
4. **Clearance demand is specific.** "verlassenschaft entrümpelung graz", "messie entrümpelung graz", "entrümpelung keller graz", "wohnungsauflösung graz", "räumungsunternehmen graz", "haushaltsauflösung steiermark" → separate pages for Entrümpelung, Haushaltsauflösung/Verlassenschaft and Messiewohnung.
5. **Local logistics questions.** "halteverbot umzug graz" → covered in the checklist (Straßenamt Graz, official link).
6. **Route searches** ("umzugsfirma graz wien", "möbeltransport graz wien", "übersiedlung wien graz") → Austria-wide availability "nach Absprache" stated on every relevant page; no thin city pages created.

## On-page implementation

- Unique `<title>` and meta description per URL (enforced by `tests/e2e/seo.spec.ts`).
- Primary keyword in title, H1 and intro of the mapped page; secondary terms in H2s, FAQs and body where natural.
- Each service page: direct-answer block ("Auf einen Blick"), scope, audience, typical jobs, process, preparation, quote info, pricing logic, add-ons, service area, FAQs, related articles.
- Each article links to 2–3 service pages and to related articles with varied anchor text.
- Structured data: `MovingCompany` (Organization/LocalBusiness subtype), `WebSite`, `Service` per service page, `BlogPosting` per article, `BreadcrumbList` everywhere, `ItemList` on the hub. No FAQ markup (Google restricts FAQ rich results to authoritative government/health sites since 2023; visible FAQs remain).

## Ranking factors outside the website

Position one cannot be promised. Beyond this site, rankings in Graz depend on: Google Business Profile (category "Umzugsunternehmen", service area instead of address if no customer visits, real photos, services, reviews), genuine reviews over time, consistent NAP on directories (WKO Firmen A–Z, Herold, Google, Apple Maps), local backlinks (partners, Hausverwaltungen, local press), content freshness, and real user signals.
